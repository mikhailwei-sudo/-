# Great Tea Road 2.0 / Великий чайный путь / 万里茶道

## Purpose
Trilingual (RU, ZH, EN) educational digital museum of tea trade between China, Mongolia, and Russia.

## Museum spaces
- **Kyakhta and Maimaichen**: border trade, photographs, architecture, merchants and material culture.
- **China and tea production**: Xiamei/Wuyishan, Hankou, brick-tea pressing and packaging.
- **Caravan journey**: routes through northern China and Mongolia, camels and trade goods.
- **Legacy**: samovars, tea drinking and cultural exchange.

## Technology
- Accessible mobile-first web app
- Three.js/WebGL for genuine interactive 3D exhibition
- Smooth cinematic scroll-driven transitions, with reduced-motion fallback
- Persistent RU/中文/EN language selection
- Interactive historical map with route *variants* and sources

## Source requirements
Only genuine museum images / historical images, with explicit citation and rights review. Archive all permitted display images locally. Avoid AI-generated or fabricated 'historic photos'.

## Milestones
1. Repository, structure and source inventory
2. Import the verified Stage 1 site and rights-cleared assets
3. Museum rooms and interactive vitrine models
4. Map, narration and educational interactions
5. Cross-device testing and deployment
